Creating a Brighter

Future for Orphaned Youth

Al Aman Fund for the Future of Orphans was Established in 2006 by Her Majesty Queen Rania Al Abdullah to Support Orphaned Youth After the Age of 18.

Our Story

Who We Are

Over the past two decades, Al Aman Fund has become a leading national model in supporting orphaned youth beyond the age of 18. Established as a royal initiative by Her Majesty Queen Rania Al Abdullah and officially registered as a non-profit organization in 2006, Al Aman is the first institution of its kind in Jordan dedicated to serving this critical and often overlooked demographic.
